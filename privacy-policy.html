Privacy Policy

Last revised: February 26, 2026

Dietary Network Services, LLC ("DNS," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website at dietarynetwork.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Company or organization name (optional)
  • Email address
  • Telephone number (optional)
  • Service of interest
  • Message content

We do not collect payment information. We do not collect health information.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect certain technical data automatically, including your browser type, device type, pages visited, time on page, and referring URL. This data is collected via Google Analytics only after you consent to analytics cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request for services
  • Communicate with you about your engagement with DNS

We use analytics data to understand how visitors interact with our site and to improve the user experience. We do not use analytics data for advertising or to build personal profiles.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, our legal bases for processing your personal data are:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies, which we only set after you explicitly accept them
  • Legitimate interests — to respond to inquiries you initiate by submitting our contact form

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information only with third-party services that help us operate the website:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) — processes contact form submissions and routes them to our email. Formspree stores submitted data temporarily to deliver the message. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — collects anonymous usage statistics when you consent to analytics cookies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

5. Data Retention

Email inquiries received from contact form submissions are retained for as long as necessary to respond to and fulfill your request, and for up to two years thereafter for business record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion at any time.

Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics default settings (26 months).

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy. Necessary cookies are set automatically. Analytics cookies are only set after you provide consent via our cookie banner.

7. Your Rights

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

EEA / UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

8. Data Security

Our website is served exclusively over HTTPS. We do not store personal data in our website's codebase or on our web server. Contact form submissions are processed by Formspree, which employs industry-standard security measures.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last revised" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
