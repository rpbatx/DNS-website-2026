Dietary Network Service – Time Tested Excellence

Who We Are

Dietary Network Services is a professional consulting company focused upon providing highly skilled, licensed dietetic professionals to organizations requiring skilled expertise in all matters relating to nutrition and diet.

Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Austin, TX, DNS has built a reputation for experienced licensed personnel, industry-trained management, and comprehensive support materials that ensure quality service delivery at every level.

We bring national relationships, deep dive expertise, combined with vast industry knowledge — providing our clients with the confidence to know they are partnered with the best in the business.

Our Philosophy

We are a caring organization, committed to the idea that we can bring about healthy changes to our end users, and those changes in turn will create balance in their lives. Our commitment to customer satisfaction is unwavering — we hold ourselves to the highest professional standards in every engagement.
