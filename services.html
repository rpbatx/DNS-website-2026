Long Term Care Facilities

DNS provides comprehensive dietary services through experienced professional consultants to long-term care facility operators and staff, offering every level of support needed to ensure your dietary department operates in a smooth, cost-effective, quality, and caring manner.

Clinical Services

Registered Dietitian consultants provide nutritional assessments, ongoing contract support, vacation coverage, and case-by-case consultations for nutritionally at-risk residents.

Survey Management

We offer pre-survey evaluations, compliance planning, and crisis management during failed inspections — including a 100% track record for survey turnarounds. Focus areas include weight loss, pressure sores, enteral feeding, sanitation, menus, food temperatures, and diet compliance.

Menu Development

DNS creates customizable menus with recipes tailored to your facility's population and budget — ensuring nutritional compliance and resident satisfaction.

Executive Consulting

Leadership receives actionable insight on dietary-related costs, risk management, and revenue generation opportunities in areas such as mechanically altered diets and enteral therapy.

Legal Case Support

DNS has provided expert witness services to the legal community for over 20 years. We specialize in cases involving malnutrition, dehydration, weight loss, and nutritional pressure sore management in long-term care settings.

Case Reviews

DNS conducts thorough medical record analysis, drawing on expertise in dietary regulations, field experience, medical knowledge, and nursing home operations to deliver clear, defensible case assessments.

Depositions

Our consultants provide expert interview and deposition services, leveraging deep industry knowledge to support legal teams with authority and confidence.

Trial Support

DNS extends support through trial conclusion, providing on-site dietary expertise when cases reach the courtroom. Clients work with the same consultant from case review through verdict.
