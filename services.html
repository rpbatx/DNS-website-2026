Our Services

Specialized dietary consulting across four key practice areas — delivered by licensed, experienced dietitians.

Legal Case Support

DNS has provided expert witness services to the legal community for over 25 years. We specialize in cases involving malnutrition, dehydration, weight loss, and nutritional pressure sore management in long-term care settings. As well as nutritional assessment situations.

Our capabilities span from case review through expert testimony. We can provide Affidavit of Merit, Case Screening, medical summary and attorney support.

Case Reviews

DNS conducts thorough medical record analysis, drawing on expertise in dietary regulations, field experience, medical knowledge, and nursing home operations to deliver clear, defensible case assessments.

Depositions

Our consultants provide expert interview and deposition services, leveraging deep industry knowledge to support legal teams with authority and confidence.

Trial Support

DNS extends support through trial conclusion, providing on-site dietary expertise when cases reach the courtroom. Clients work with the same consultant from case review through verdict.

Legal Case Support

  • 35+ years expert witness experience
  • Specializing in long-term care dietary disputes
  • Nutritional analysis
  • Same consultant throughout your case
  • Medical record analysis and case review
  • Deposition and courtroom testimony

Consultative Services

With decades of operational and management experience, DNS provides senior executives with the insight, analysis, and strategic support needed to optimize all dietary and nutritional operations.

Cost Analysis

We produce concise, easy-to-read reports that show a true reflection of food, labor, and supplement costs — giving leadership the visibility to make confident financial decisions.

Risk Management

Identify and mitigate dietary-related compliance and operational risks before they become costly problems.

Team Building

Organizational development and team training support to build high-performing dietary departments.

Revenue Generation

DNS identifies untapped revenue opportunities through areas such as referral programs for mechanically altered diets and enteral therapy management.

Consulting Deliverables

  • Risk management assessments
  • Operational optimization plans
  • Team building programs
  • Revenue opportunity identification

Long Term Care Facilities

DNS provides comprehensive dietary services through experienced professional consultants to long-term care facility operators and staff, offering every level of support needed to ensure your dietary department operates in a smooth, cost-effective, quality, and caring manner.

Clinical Services

Registered Dietitian consultants provide nutritional assessments, ongoing contract support, vacation coverage, and case-by-case consultations for nutritionally at-risk residents.

Survey Management

We offer pre-survey evaluations, compliance planning, and crisis management during failed inspections — including a 100% track record for survey turnarounds.

Menu Development

DNS creates customizable menus with recipes tailored to your facility's population and budget — ensuring nutritional compliance and resident satisfaction.

Executive Consulting

Leadership receives actionable insight on dietary-related costs, risk management, and revenue generation opportunities in areas such as mechanically altered diets and enteral therapy.

Long Term Care Facilities

  • Licensed Registered Dietitians only
  • 100% survey turnaround track record
  • Flexible — ongoing contracts or case-by-case
  • Vacation and interim coverage available
  • Customized menus and care plans
