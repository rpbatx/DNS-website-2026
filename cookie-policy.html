Cookie Policy

Last revised: February 26, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Dietary Network Services, LLC ("DNS," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website at dietarynetwork.com. By using our site, you agree to our use of cookies as described below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember certain information about your visit. Some cookies are stored only for the duration of your browser session ("session cookies"); others remain on your device for a set period ("persistent cookies").

We also use browser localStorage — a similar technology that stores data locally on your device — to remember your cookie consent preference.

2. Cookies We Use

We use two categories of cookies on this site:

Necessary (Always Active)

These cookies are essential for the site to function and cannot be disabled.

Name Type Purpose Duration
dns_cookie_consent localStorage Stores your cookie consent preference ("accepted" or "rejected") so we do not show the banner on every page visit. Persistent (until cleared by user)

Analytics (Requires Consent)

These cookies are only set after you click "Accept All" on our cookie banner. They help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Name Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors by assigning a randomly generated number. Used to calculate visit, session, and campaign data for site analytics. 2 years
_ga_<ID> Google Analytics Used to persist session state for Google Analytics 4. 2 years

Analytics data collected by Google Analytics is anonymous — it does not identify you personally. DNS does not combine analytics data with any personal information you provide via our contact form.

3. How We Obtain Your Consent

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner appears at the bottom of the screen. You have two choices:

  • Accept All — enables both necessary and analytics cookies
  • Necessary Only — enables only the cookies required for the site to function; analytics cookies are not set

Your choice is remembered in your browser's localStorage under the key dns_cookie_consent. If you clear your browser data, the banner will appear again on your next visit.

4. How to Manage or Withdraw Consent

You can change or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the following methods:

  • Clear site data in your browser — Go to your browser's privacy or developer settings and clear localStorage or site data for dietarynetwork.com. The consent banner will reappear on your next visit.
  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings menus. Note that blocking all cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.
  • Google Analytics opt-out — You can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics across all websites.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Google Analytics cookies are set by Google LLC. DNS does not control these cookies directly. For more information on how Google uses data collected through its analytics services, see Google's partner site policy.

No other third-party advertising, tracking, or social media cookies are used on this site.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The "Last revised" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
